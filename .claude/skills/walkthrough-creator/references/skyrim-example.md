# The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim — Completionist Walkthrough

A comprehensive checklist for completionist coverage, organized as a linear geographic path with quest chains tagged for easy tracking. Covers all major questlines, guilds, DLC, Daedric artifacts, and collectibles.

---

## Helgen & Riverwood

- [ ] `Main` Complete "Unbound" — escape Helgen with Ralof (Stormcloak) or Hadvar (Imperial)
- [ ] `Collectible` Activate one of the Guardian Stones (Warrior/Mage/Thief) on the road to Riverwood
- [ ] `Main` Speak to Alvor (if Hadvar) or Gerdur (if Ralof) in Riverwood
- [ ] `Main` Begin "Before the Storm" — travel to Whiterun to warn the Jarl

## Whiterun — First Visit

- [ ] `Main` Meet Jarl Balgruuf the Greater in Dragonsreach
- [ ] `Main` Complete "Bleak Falls Barrow" — retrieve the Dragonstone for Farengar Secret-Fire
- [ ] `Main` Complete "Dragon Rising" — defeat the dragon at the Western Watchtower and absorb your first dragon soul
- [ ] `Main` Begin "The Way of the Voice" — summoned to High Hrothgar by the Greybeards
- [ ] `Companions` Visit Jorrvaskr and witness the fight in the yard
- [ ] `Companions` Complete "Take Up Arms" — join the Companions (speak to Kodlak Whitemane)
- [ ] `Daedric` "The Whispering Door" — speak to Hulda at the Bannered Mare about rumours, then speak to Jarl Balgruuf (requires level 20)
- [ ] `Side` "The Blessings of Nature" — speak to Danica Pure-Spring at the Temple of Kynareth
- [ ] `Side` "Missing in Action" — speak to Fralia Gray-Mane at the market
- [ ] `Collectible` Stone of Barenziah — Dragonsreach, Jarl's bedroom
- [ ] `Collectible` Stone of Barenziah — Jorrvaskr, Kodlak's bedroom
- [ ] `Collectible` Stone of Barenziah — Hall of the Dead, Whiterun

## Completionist Notes

1. **Nahkriin is missable.** You cannot return to Skuldafn after the main quest. Get it during "The World-Eater's Eyrie."

2. **Stone of Barenziah at the Thalmor Embassy is missable.** Grab it during "Diplomatic Immunity."

3. **Civil War is mutually exclusive.** You cannot complete both sides in one playthrough.

4. **Daedric artifact choices matter for "Oblivion Walker."** You need 15 artifacts. Key choices: take Masque of Clavicus Vile (not Rueful Axe), take Skull of Corruption (don't spare Erandur), take both Savior's Hide AND Ring of Hircine from "Ill Met by Moonlight" (possible with specific timing).

5. **"Destroy the Dark Brotherhood"** is an alternative to joining. Completing the full questline and the destruction path are mutually exclusive.

6. **DLC triggers:** Dawnguard cultists appear after level 10. Dragonborn cultists appear after completing "The Way of the Voice."

7. **Paarthurnax dilemma:** The Blades refuse to help you further unless you kill Paarthurnax. This is optional and has no impact on the main quest.
